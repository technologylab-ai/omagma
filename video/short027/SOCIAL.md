# v0.2.7 video copy

## Video title

Omagma v0.2.7 — 9 highlights 🌋

## Video description

Eight visible workflow upgrades, with time to follow each action:

1. **Start from your bar:** launch the TUI from the Omarchy popup.
2. **Find a command:** search the contextual action palette.
3. **Find inside the message:** move between highlighted matches.
4. **Choose labels. Apply once.** Stage changes and see the result.
5. **Meetings, made readable:** friendly details and o Join from review.
6. **Pick files together:** attach the complete selected set.
7. **Forward with a personal note:** keep the original HTML beneath your note.
8. **Send with a safety net:** Undo during the countdown, before submission.

Also improved: searchable Help, saved searches, label colors, named recipients,
Omagma/Follow Omarchy themes, multi-address contacts, sender choice, native
text undo/redo, unread jumps, half-page navigation, archiving one message or
its whole thread, Spam/Not spam, safe local draft discard, attachment reminders,
Save All/selected, keyboard-accessible dialogs, stable triage and explicit
Resume/Cancel for paused sends. Cleaner rendering, readable mail status and
missing-recipient checks complete the everyday polish. The CLI shares the
corresponding mail workflows.

The TUI and CLI are experimental on Linux and macOS. The Omarchy bar stays
Linux-only and read-only. The film uses fictional accounts and messages.
