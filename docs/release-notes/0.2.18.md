# Omagma 0.2.18

Ask Google Meet to take AI meeting notes and a transcript when creating a new
Calendar event.

- Paste a clipboard image with Ctrl+V or `:paste-image`. In a Markdown body it's
  placed at the cursor, and recipients see it in place in HTML mail. In Plain
  bodies and other fields it's attached normally.
- Scroll send review with the mouse wheel.
- Resizing the terminal redraws Omagma at the new size, keeping your draft,
  focus, and any open dialog.
- One **Auto Meet AI notes + transcript** checkbox beside Google Meet requests
  both features for new events on the organizing account's primary calendar.
  Leaving it unchecked sends no Meet setting; Google's defaults still apply.
- Review the event before saving. Omagma saves the event, configures its Meet
  link, and confirms success only after reading both settings back from Google.
  Delayed links, refusals and uncertain outcomes stay visible; checking a status
  never repeats event creation or automatically resends a settings request.
- Optional **meet-settings** access preserves the account's existing permissions
  and credential store during reauthorization. Enable the Google Meet REST API
  in the same Cloud project. Google also requires a Workspace plan and admin
  policy that allow AI notes, plus its meeting participation and consent rules.
- Calendar Week shows a red current-time line across today’s column, including
  occupied and selected meeting rows, without obscuring their text. It follows
  the displayed time zone and existing clock ticks, with a red `now` label in
  the time gutter.
- Release assets now cover Linux (x86_64, arm64) and macOS on Apple Silicon (arm64); Intel Mac builds are no longer published, and [v0.2.17](https://github.com/technologylab-ai/omagma/releases/tag/v0.2.17) remains the last release with an Intel download.
- Mail reading help and the usage skill explain that `bodyHtml` is optional
  and can be present alongside `bodySource: "plain"`; plain text is the preferred
  reading source, rather than proof that the message has no HTML alternative.

The Meet option applies to new events only. Omagma does not join or monitor
meetings and cannot guarantee capture after a disconnect. Check the saved
status and Google's in-meeting controls if setup was refused or unconfirmed.

Restart open clients after updating. The **experimental** native app supports
Linux and macOS; the bar remains read-only and the browser remains a tech demo.
