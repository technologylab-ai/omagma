# Omagma 0.2.11

A small update to account-connection diagnostics in the terminal.

- Expired or revoked Google sign-in now clearly asks you to reconnect the
  affected account when sending mail or continuing a draft in Gmail.
- Rejected sends and RSVP submissions show readable explanations alongside
  their diagnostic codes. Unsent drafts are retained.
- Regression tests cover rejected OAuth refreshes, draft preservation and
  explicit send/upload after reconnecting, with no automatic submission.

If Google rejects your sign-in, [reconnect the terminal account](https://github.com/technologylab-ai/omagma/blob/v0.2.11/docs/SETUP.md#full-tuicli-permissions),
then review your retained draft before trying again.

The experimental native TUI and CLI run on Linux and macOS. The Omarchy bar
remains read-only; the bundled browser tech demo retains its existing scope.
