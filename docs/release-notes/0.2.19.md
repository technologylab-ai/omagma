# Omagma 0.2.19

A proper calendar grid, quicker guest selection and a Calendar look that matches
Omagma's orange, blue and white Mail interface.

- **Day and Week grids:** events are blocks on a time axis, with side-by-side
  overlaps, an all-day band, working-hours framing and the red current-time
  indicator. Scroll with Ctrl+E/Ctrl+Y; hidden overlaps remain selectable.
- **Orange & Blue Calendar look:** white event text, vivid source markers and
  cool blue surfaces. Ctrl+T in Agenda, Day or Week restores Classic or switches
  back; the choice is remembered. Mail, Follow Omarchy and monochrome keep their
  existing appearance and each calendar keeps its saved colour identity.
- **Guest suggestions while typing:** after two letters, choose from the
  destination account's cached Contacts and recent mail. Nothing is selected
  automatically. Down/Ctrl+N chooses; Enter/Tab adds. Ctrl+Space opens Browse all
  with the partial name already entered, replacing just that entry. Typing does
  not search Google or send invitations.
- **Delete and RSVP in Calendar:** D or r opens a review naming the event,
  calendar, occurrence or series, and who receives an email. A deliberate
  confirmation applies a version-checked change. Unconfirmed outcomes remain
  recoverable and are checked without automatically repeating the request.
- **Personal events from emailed ICS files:** review and add attendee-free
  published events, including uppercase .ICS files labelled text/plain. Preserve
  all-day dates, notes and supported popup reminders, with unsupported alarms
  disclosed. Existing meeting invitations and RSVP flows remain separate.
- **Discard drafts with undo:** Ctrl+X reviews a discard from any compose state.
  Ctrl+Z immediately afterwards, or :restore-draft after restarting, restores the
  last discarded draft for that account, for up to seven days. Untouched new
  composers keep no draft. Gmail drafts and protected sends retain their guards.
- **Reconnect an expired account in the TUI:** Ctrl+O or :reconnect shows Google's
  refusal reason and retained permissions before opening consent in the
  account's Chrome profile. A successful reconnect refreshes mail and calendars.
  Calendar names unavailable accounts without marking other calendars incomplete.
- **Mail polish:** clearer dates and recipient names in Sent, aligned reader
  fields and friendly label chips, contextual navigation hints, unread jumps
  with n/N, and g then i/s/d/a/t for mailboxes. Escape backs out without quitting.
- **More resilient reading:** unusual headers, character sets and individual
  calendar properties degrade locally with a notice instead of hiding the whole
  message or view. Stale decoding refusals are retried once. Replies remain
  usable with untidy threading headers or an unusual send-as alias.
- **Terminal repaint fixes:** emoji-width negotiation and full repainting keep
  Mail → Calendar → Mail transitions aligned, including formatted-view fallbacks.
- **Agent and CLI improvements:** complete new-event Meet AI notes examples,
  current-recipient guidance, compact command help, strict unknown-field errors,
  supported dry runs, smaller read projections, one-call replies and recipient-
  preserving draft patches. Exit codes distinguish refusal and unknown outcomes.

Calendar edits and invitations still require review and confirmation. Meet AI
notes and transcription apply to new meetings and depend on Google Workspace
eligibility, administrator policy and Google's meeting participation rules.

A Calendar write journal created by 0.2.19 is refused by 0.2.18 rather than
misread. Kept discarded drafts do not appear in 0.2.18's Drafts list and cannot
be restored there.

Restart open clients after updating. Omagma's native terminal app remains
**experimental**, supports Linux and Apple Silicon macOS, and keeps the bar
read-only. The browser remains a tech demo.
